Child safety standards

Effective 27 October 2026 · TableTrack (com.olderwold.gm) on Android, Android TV and iOS

We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Such content and conduct are prohibited in TableTrack. We report them to the authorities and cooperate fully with law enforcement.

1. What is prohibited

Using TableTrack, including its chat, character portraits, maps, notes and any shared file, to create, store, share or promote any of the following is strictly prohibited:

  • child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind, including drawn, generated or edited imagery;
  • content that sexualises minors, or that depicts, encourages or normalises the abuse of children;
  • grooming: contacting or manipulating a minor in order to abuse or exploit them;
  • sextortion, or any threat or coercion aimed at obtaining sexual images or acts from a minor;
  • child trafficking, or any arrangement of contact with a minor for sexual purposes;
  • any other conduct that sexually exploits, abuses or endangers children.

Harassment, bullying, threats and hateful content are also prohibited at any table.

2. How the app is designed

  • No strangers. TableTrack has no public profiles, no search for other users, no friend lists and no public spaces. A player can only reach a table that a Dungeon Master hosts on the same local Wi-Fi network, using a code the Dungeon Master gives them in person.
  • No servers. We do not host, relay or store any user content. Chat messages and pictures travel only between the devices at one table and stay on those devices.
  • Control at the table. The Dungeon Master can delete any chat message and release any seat, which disconnects that player. A player can leave a table at any time with “Forget this table”. A TableCast screen shows nothing until the Dungeon Master allows it.
  • Not for young children. The app is not directed at children under 13.

3. How to report a concern

If you see or suspect child abuse, exploitation or any CSAM connected with TableTrack, report it:

  • To us: email tom.koptel@gmail.com with the subject “Child safety report”. Describe what happened and when. Never attach or forward abusive images; describe them instead.
  • To the authorities: if a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police or emergency number first. You can also report to the NCMEC CyberTipline (United States), the Internet Watch Foundation (United Kingdom), or a hotline in your country through INHOPE.

4. How we respond

  • We acknowledge child safety reports within 24 hours and act on them as our first priority.
  • Where we obtain actual knowledge of CSAM, we report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to the relevant authorities in line with applicable law, and preserve what the law requires us to preserve.
  • We cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations. Because we hold no user data or content, we may be unable to provide material ourselves, but we will help with everything we can.
  • Where an account, listing or distribution channel we control is misused, we remove access. We also report abuse to Google Play and Apple as the distributors of the app.

5. Compliance

We comply with applicable child safety laws and regulations in the countries where TableTrack is distributed, and with the Google Play Child Safety Standards policy and Apple’s App Store Review Guidelines.
