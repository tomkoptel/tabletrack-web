Hosting a table: the foreground service

Why TableTrack uses FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CONNECTED_DEVICE (continuous data transfer to an external device). The Dungeon Master’s phone, left, hosts the table; an Android TV running TableCast, right, connects over the local Wi-Fi:

  1. The TV asks to show the table, and the Dungeon Master allows it.
  2. The Dungeon Master reveals the battle map, and the TV shows it with the creature tokens.
  3. The Dungeon Master leaves the app. The hosting service keeps serving the table in the background, shown by the ongoing “Hosting The Sunken Keep” notification, and the TV stays live.
  4. The Dungeon Master taps Stop on the notification. Hosting ends, the notification goes, and the TV reports it has lost the table.
