Privacy policy

Effective 27 October 2026 · Applies to the TableTrack app (package com.olderwold.gm) on Android, Android TV and iOS, including its TableTrack, TablePlay and TableCast modes.

In short: we do not collect, receive, sell or share any of your data. TableTrack has no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no analytics. What you create stays on your device. When you play together, the devices at your table talk to each other directly over your own Wi-Fi, and nothing goes through us.

1. Who we are

TableTrack is developed by Tom Koptel, publishing as Olderwold (“we”, “us”). For any question about this policy or your data, write to tom.koptel@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Nothing. The app does not send personal data, usage data, device identifiers, location or crash reports to us or to any analytics or advertising service. We run no server that could receive it.

3. What the app stores on your device

To do its job, the app keeps the following in its private storage on your device, where other apps cannot read it:

  • Campaigns, adventures, encounters, notes and treasure you create
  • Characters you build, and any creatures, spells and items you add
  • Pictures you choose for maps, campaigns and portraits
  • Messages in the table chat between a player and the Dungeon Master
  • For a table you host: its name, the seats and a log of the changes made during play
  • Your settings

This data never leaves your device unless you send it, as described in sections 4 and 5. Each mode (TableTrack, TablePlay, TableCast) keeps its own separate data.

4. Playing together on your Wi-Fi

When the Dungeon Master hosts a table, the host device runs a small service on your local network. Players' devices and a TableCast screen on the same network connect to it directly. Depending on what the Dungeon Master shares, the devices exchange:

  • the table's name and the characters' sheets, the party and the state of a fight;
  • map pictures the Dungeon Master chooses to show, with the grid and tokens;
  • chat messages between a player and the Dungeon Master;
  • a device name (for example “TV Stick”), so the Dungeon Master can see which screen asks to join.

This traffic stays on your local network and does not pass through the internet or through us. It is not encrypted, so anyone able to watch your Wi-Fi traffic could read it: play on a network you trust. Each seat is joined with its own link or QR code, so share codes only with your players. A TableCast screen shows nothing until the Dungeon Master allows it.

5. When you share something

If you choose to export a backup, a campaign or a character, the app hands the file to your device’s own share sheet or file picker, and it goes where you send it. What happens to it there is governed by the app or service you pick.

6. Permissions

PermissionWhy
Local network / nearby devices, internet, network stateTo host or join a table and find a TableCast screen on your Wi-Fi. The app makes no connections to the internet.
Foreground service, notificationsTo keep a hosted table running while the screen is off, and to show the notification that lets you stop it.
Camera (iOS)To scan the QR code the Dungeon Master shows. Frames are read on the device and never stored or sent.

On Android the app does not hold the camera permission: QR codes are scanned by Google Play services’ code scanner, which returns only the scanned text to the app. Pictures are chosen through the system photo picker, so the app sees only the pictures you pick, not your gallery.

7. Third parties

  • Google Play delivers the app and its updates on Android. Google’s handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play services code scanner (ML Kit) scans QR codes on Android. It runs on the device; Google may collect limited diagnostic and usage information about it, as described in the ML Kit terms.
  • Apple’s App Store delivers the app on iOS, under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

The app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs. If the app crashes, it shows the error on your screen; it is not sent anywhere unless you choose to send it to us.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep it. You can delete campaigns, characters and messages in the app, and uninstalling the app removes all of its data from the device. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete, but you are welcome to contact us with any request.

9. Security

App data lives in storage private to the app, protected by your device’s own security. Table connections are limited to your local network, and each seat needs its own code. Please keep your device locked and play on networks you trust.

10. Children

TableTrack is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. Because the app collects nothing, no child’s data reaches us. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it. Our standards against child abuse are on the child safety page.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA in California), you have rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal data and to object to its processing. Since we do not collect or process personal data, we hold none to provide; your data is on your device and under your control. We do not sell or share personal information. You may contact us, or your local data protection authority, at any time.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change ever means the app would collect data, we will say so in the app before it happens.
